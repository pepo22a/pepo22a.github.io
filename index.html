About the Premier League

The English Premier League (EPL) is the top tier of English soccer, featuring 20 clubs competing for the championship each season. It is cosidered by many as the best league in the world.

The Competition History

The English Premier League started in 1992 when the top clubs left the old First Division to start their own league. Manchester United won the first title, and they were really tough to beat with Alex Ferguson in charge. They won 13 league titles since 1992, making them the most successful team in the Premier League era. There is been some crazy moments over the years, like Leicester winning the league in 2016 when nobody really seen it coming. It is the best league in the world because the games are exciting, there is so many great players, and any team can beat any team.
