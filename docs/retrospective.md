# Project Retrospective

## What Went Well?

Choosing the English Premier League was a good decision because I already enjoy soccer. It made writing the pages more interesting, and I didn't have to spend too much time trying to find a topic. 

Getting quality information also went well. I found useful details that helped me decide what to put on each page. This part was easier than I expected.
## What Went Wrong?

Time management was probably my biggest problem. I thought the website would take less time, but writing, coding, and organizing everything took longer than I planned. My nerve pain and difficulty typing also slowed me down, so some days I couldn't get as much done as I wanted.

I also got confused about where the files should go. I had other assignments in the same repository, and at first I wasn't sure which files belonged in the main folder. Moving them around was a little confusing too, because the links depends on where the files are saved.

## What Would I Do Differently?

Next time I would start earlier and do a little bit each day. I would plan shorter work sessions around my spinal cord injury and severe nerve pain, and take breaks when I need them. Since typing takes me longer, I would give myself extra time. On days when the pain is worse, I would also contact my instructor earlier if my health starts affecting deadlines

I would also organize the folders before I start coding. Keeping the website files together and putting the documents in the docs folder would save me some confusion. I learned a lot from this project, even though it took more time then I expected.
